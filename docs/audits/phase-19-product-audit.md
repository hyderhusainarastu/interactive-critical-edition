**Register note (2026-07-23) — a standing CI blind spot this exposed.** `BETA_TESTING_MODE` is off in CI and on in production, so *nothing beta-gated is ever rendered in a CI run*: the badge, the closed-registration path, and the `BetaNotice` panel are all invisible to every automated check. D-23-21 reached production through exactly that gap. Turning the flag on in CI is not a fix — it would close registration and break `auth.spec.ts`'s real signup test. The workable mitigations are the one applied here (assert the underlying token/behaviour in a flag-independent way) and, for anything that cannot be reduced to that, an explicit post-deploy production check. Any future beta-gated UI should assume CI will not see it.
