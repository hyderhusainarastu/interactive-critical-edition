Active phase · 6 of 8

Interactive Learning Workspace

9.7 next

Phase 9 turns a trustworthy critical edition into a space a reader can learn in: level-aware without withholding depth, passage-grounded, and organized around what to read and understand next.

  1. 9.1 · Foundations & flag

    Version-aware v1/v2/v3 pipeline handling and schema foundations for reader levels, concepts, canonical works, Library resources, and roles.

  2. 9.2 · Research sequence v3

    Creator evidence and separated credibility dimensions; popularity is visible but never counted as credibility. Verified by a production canary.

  3. 9.3 · Passage annotations

    Short, expandable annotations anchored to real text blocks, with whole-work guidance kept distinct. Verified by a production canary.

  4. 9.4 · Reader levels & mastery

    Four reader levels, optional self-assessment, and a protected mastery precedence chain. Verified by a production canary.

  5. 9.5 · Library & work grouping

    Separate uploaded works from recommended sources; Library states, filters, sorts, and navigation. Verified by a production canary.

  6. 9.6 · Curriculum & study guide

    Minimal, university, and graduate routes with acyclic dependencies, rationales, checkpoints, and review-only completion. No production canary needed — pure read-time compute over 9.5's data.

  7. 9.7 · Graph, trash & cost UI

    Expanded graph entities and parity across 3D/table views, 30-day restoreable trash, and transparent cost controls.

  8. 9.8 · Comprehensive dossier

    Fourteen deeper modules behind an $8 warning and $20 confirmed ceiling.

What “complete” means for this phase
TrustEvery annotation is genuinely anchored; a failed v3 publish leaves the previous edition served.
ConsistencyReader, Library, Curriculum, and Graph share canonical IDs; 3D and table filters return the same set.
Reader controlState persists; Library changes affect Curriculum and Graph; cost caps, trash, restore, and purge are safe.
